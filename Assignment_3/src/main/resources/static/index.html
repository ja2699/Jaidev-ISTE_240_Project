About our Library

Readers Public Library has been serving the community since 1984. We offer a wide selection of fiction, non-fiction, academic texts and digital resources. Membership is free and open everyone.

Borrowing Policy

Members may borrow up to 3 books at a time for a period of 3 weeks. Renewals are available once per borrowing period. Late returns will incur a small fee that adds up for each day youre late.
